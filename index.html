Why "Delta-V"?

The push to reach a higher orbit.

In spaceflight, delta-v is the change in velocity a spacecraft needs to move to a higher orbit. It is planned carefully and spent wisely. That is how we think about growing a business: a measured push, at the right moment, without changing what makes the company work.
