Our approach to partnership

Every partnership is built around the owner.

No two businesses or owners are the same, so we don't use a one-size-fits-all structure.

We start by understanding your goals for yourself, your team, and the company. From there, we work together to find the structure that fits, whether that means a full transition or a partnership that helps the business grow while you stay involved.

Flexible structures

Ways we work with owners

The right structure depends on where you are and where you want the company to go.

01

Acquisition

For owners who are ready for their next chapter. We acquire the business and work with you on a thoughtful, well-planned transition.

02

Growth investment

For owners who want to keep building. We invest in the company and work alongside you to help it reach its next stage of growth.

03

Tailored partnership

For situations in between. We're open to structures that fit your goals, your timeline, and your company.

What we bring

More than capital

  • Operational support in systems, processes, and program management
  • A long-term view, with patience to do things the right way
